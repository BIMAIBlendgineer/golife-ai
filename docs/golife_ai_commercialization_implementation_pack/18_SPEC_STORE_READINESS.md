# SPEC — Store Readiness

## Apple review readiness
- complete metadata;
- tested app;
- contact info;
- demo account/mode if needed;
- backend live for review;
- IAP complete if offered;
- accurate privacy details.

## Google Play readiness
- Data Safety form;
- privacy policy;
- Google Play Billing for in-app digital goods/services unless exception applies;
- accurate pricing terms;
- deletion mechanism disclosure;
- screenshots/listing accuracy.

## GoLife-specific copy restrictions
- no therapy;
- no medical/legal/financial advice;
- no enterprise-ready claim;
- release locales declared;
- fallback/degraded behavior documented.
